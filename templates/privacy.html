Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide Spendly's expense tracking features, we collect the information you give us directly, such as your name and email address when you create an account, and the expense records you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We also collect basic technical data, like login timestamps, needed to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide solely to operate and improve the Service. This includes:

  • Displaying your expenses, categories, and spending summaries back to you.
  • Authenticating your account and keeping it secure.
  • Diagnosing bugs and improving the reliability of the Service.

We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in Spendly's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which it will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers — such as hosting and email delivery services — solely to operate the Service, and only to the extent needed for them to perform that function on our behalf.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at support@spendly.app.
